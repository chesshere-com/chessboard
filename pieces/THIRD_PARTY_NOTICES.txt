ChessHere bundled chess piece sets

Directory and file names are packaging choices.
Cburnett XML document type declarations were removed to avoid external DTD references.
Flat CC0, Font Awesome Chess, and Material Design Chess were recolored and given
contrasting outlines to produce separate white and black piece files.
License texts are in the LICENSES directory.
